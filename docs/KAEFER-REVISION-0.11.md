# Beetle revision — design brief and acceptance contract

Approved 21 October 2026. This release revises the existing forty caves. Spiders and webs belong to a separate chapter 41–50 design. DW in 25 remains sealed.

## Rule contract

Rules v6: six seconds in at most two reachable cells arms a 3×3 explosion; access to three cells resets it. Creatures do not constitute cage walls. Diggers can escape through earth and bedrock. Larger marked enclosures remain live-capture objectives. Red warning takes precedence over metamorphosis. Blasts remove earth, ordinary masonry, rocks and creatures; gems, keys, steel, bedrock and reinforced devices survive. No secondary chain explosions and no explosion-produced diamonds.

The third oriented visit starts a twelve-tick warning: blue/purple exchange handedness; amber/white exchange digging and diamond-drop abilities. Actual excavation resets the digger's loop memory. A transformation first selects a least-used available departure, then restarts its memory. Golden carriers change handedness only and cannot fuse. Keys are released on crushing or explosion, fall without rolling or harm, and are not quota points. Finite pit stock never refills.

## Acceptance

Every changed cave needs a timed replay, varied starting phases, omission counter-runs and optimistic reachability checks for each claimed required action. Recheck after adding every connection. Alternative outcomes are allowed only when documented as alternatives. No hidden objective flag may conceal a map bypass. Key layouts must be integrated into their cave's existing route decision, not pasted as identical rooms.

Scree Sea must have multiple complete extraction routes and zero remaining gems. Reachability sampling is not a proof for every possible player mistake. Report actual measured automated times separately from user records and first-play feedback. New deadlines are rounded down to five seconds at no more than three times the fastest measured solution; only records of the same revision may tighten them to twice the human record.

Archive v0.10 maps and published replays; keep old rule execution unchanged. Changed hashes receive new rankings, while old records, offline proofs and unlocks survive. Verify full-state checkpoint resumes, 30/60/144-FPS schedules, server chunks, EN/DE hints, desktop and touch views. Human play feel remains a separate acceptance step.

## Implemented refinements after counter-runs

- **05:** The compact loop demonstrates a first change after 6.3 seconds. A separate observation replay waits for that change and completes in 7.9 seconds, inside the 10-second limit. The branch remains a real departure, unlike 06's deliberately tiny blast pen.
- **09:** An earth-supported ambush allowed the amber form to undermine its own projectile before the player arrived. A closed observation arena with a single-shot R1 dispenser prevents this start-time failure. This previews a dispenser before 13; 13's new problem remains coordinated fusion and controlled supply. The entrance rock is pushed aside after the hit.
- **16:** OUT is an optional outer safety gate while the inner capture corridor is prepared. P/E closes the actual enclosure; its steel boundary also holds a later amber form. Killing the live captive remains invalid.
- **26:** Carrier access initially bypassed the shared falling-boulder task. The upper and lower connections have been sealed; operator, carrier and recovery track now all lie beyond the original choke. The rock still lands in the lower shared gallery. Current proof: 71.5 seconds.
- **30/33:** Small passing bays provide an escape from a late patrol without removing the existing return commitment. The authoring route waits for completed excavation rather than one easily missed enemy position.
- **34:** G2 redirects the same worker and closes the old seam behind it. G3 gives a safe shorter return and closes its terminal pen; G3 is an optional convenience, not a claimed mandatory objective. The faster verified alternative wins the sun key before the second work stage. Both bedrock seams remain structurally required.
- **35:** G1 → R1 blast/key → G2 fusion → R2 diamond. The first carrier's alcove is bounded above so the player cannot use its approach as a bypass around the masonry. Each device has one distinct labelled purpose.
- **38:** Stopping P1 starts P2's timer. P2's shutdown is reached after its own seam and also seals its terminal worker loop. This prevents long initial waits from destroying the key setup before arrival. Both generated workers have demonstrated access duties; both protected exit locks remain necessary.
- **40:** G3 is a safety enclosure for the one worker's white phase. It does not create additional ammunition or workers. Each of the two intended white kills produces one required diamond.
